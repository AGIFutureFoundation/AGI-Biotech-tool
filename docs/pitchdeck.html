01 · THE PROBLEM

Discovery software assumes a human is driving

A chemist moves between a structure viewer, a docking program, six database websites and a spreadsheet. Each hand-off loses context, and none of it records why a decision was made. The thing most likely to do that work next is an agent, and almost no scientific software can be called by one.

02 · THE PRODUCT

One workspace, three ways in

By hand

A 3D workspace in a browser or a headset. Pick up a protein, drop a compound in its pocket, pull it about while the physics runs.

By voice

"Load LRRK2." "Find pockets." "Dock it." The answer is spoken back, which is what works on glasses with no display.

By agent

Every panel action is a typed tool. An MCP client lists them and drives a live session, the same buttons a researcher presses.

05 · ARCHITECTURE

Built so another agent can be the customer

  • Tool layer. 22 operations with JSON schemas; one registry serves console, voice and MCP.
  • x402 payments. Metered tools answer an unpaid call with a spec-shaped HTTP 402 challenge. Settlement not yet wired.
  • NANDA discovery. A signed AgentFacts document at a well-known URL, limits included.
  • OML-style fingerprints. Secret challenge/response pairs; every result signed.
  • Provenance chain. SHA-256 over every run; alter one record and verification names it.
06 · ROADMAP

What exists, and what comes next

  • Done. Workspace and data spine; agent surface with voice, hands and MCP.
  • 4 weeks. Scoring that passes its own benchmark, published with every release.
  • 6 weeks. Hands and voice tested on Quest 3 and Vision Pro; shared rooms with object ownership.
  • Quarter. x402 settlement, AgentFacts on a public index, hosted and on-premises deployment.
07 · HONEST LIMITS

What this is not, yet

  • Docking is triage, not prediction.
  • Interactive physics is coarse by design; the OpenMM backend is the one to quote.
  • Payments do not settle. A hash chain is not a blockchain.
  • Ray-Ban Meta glasses cannot render this; the voice companion is the substitute.
  • Hand tracking is untested on hardware.
The ask

Three kinds of partner
