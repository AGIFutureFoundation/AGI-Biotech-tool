The docking benchmark, reported honestly

Eleven crystal ligands pulled from their structures and docked back blind. The benchmark separates the two ways a case fails, because they call for opposite work.

5 of 11 succeed; 9 of 11 are reachable. In four failures the search found the crystal pose and the scoring function ranked something wrong above it. Perfect ranking over poses already being produced would take this from 45% to 82% — so the next work belongs in the energy terms, not the search. That is a measurement, not an intuition.

What this is not

Most of the harm a tool like this can do is by sounding more certain than it is. So, plainly:

  • Docking scores are estimates, not measurements. Not kcal/mol, not validated against experiment, and not a replacement for Vina or Glide.
  • Single runs are draws. The same case scored 1.44 Å and 7.50 Å on consecutive runs. Eleven cases at three runs cannot resolve anything below a three-case swing — which is why runs are now seedable.
  • Some numbers are placeholders, and they say so. Where no model has run, values print with a [SYNTHETIC] marker and carry a provenance field. The on-chain anchor refuses to anchor them.
  • Not a clinical or regulatory product, not a medical device, and no partnership or endorsement exists with any organisation in the disease areas it targets.
