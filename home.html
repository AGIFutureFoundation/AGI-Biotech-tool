Molecular research you can check.

Fetch a real protein structure, look at it in 3D or in VR with your team, dock a compound against it, and export a record whose every number can be traced back to where it came from — or marked plainly as a placeholder if it has not been measured.

What you can actually do today

Every item here works now. Things that do not work yet are in the section below, not hidden in a roadmap.

Load any known structure

By gene, PDB id, UniProt accession or disease. Experimental structures from the RCSB PDB, predicted ones from AlphaFold, with per-residue confidence shown rather than flattened away.

Find pockets and dock

Pocket detection and a Vina-style scoring function that runs in the browser, with a matching Python implementation checked term-by-term against it so the two cannot silently disagree.

Work in the same room

Join a room and see where other people are looking and what they are holding, on a laptop or in a headset. WebXR, no install.

Keep a record that holds up

Every run is chained by hash, so the history cannot be edited afterwards without breaking it. Export it, or anchor its root on-chain if you need a timestamp someone else can verify.

What this is not

Most of the harm a tool like this can do is by sounding more certain than it is. So, plainly:

  • Docking scores are estimates, not measurements. The in-browser score is useful for ranking and for teaching. It is not kcal/mol and it has not been validated against experiment.
  • Some numbers are placeholders, and they say so. Where no model has run, values print with a [SYNTHETIC] marker and carry a provenance field. They are not results and the software will not let them be anchored on-chain as if they were.
  • Nothing here is a clinical or regulatory product. It is not a medical device, it gives no medical advice, and a compound being listed by a supplier is not a clearance to obtain or handle it.
  • A signature is not a peer review. Signing a record proves who made a claim about it. It does not establish that the claim is true.

Teaching & simulators

Provenance you can hand to someone else

Content-addressed storage, Merkle roots, optional on-chain anchoring, and sourcing documents that separate what a vendor lists from what has been verified.

Common questions

Do I need a headset?
No. Everything works with a mouse. A headset adds room-scale and hand tracking.
Does my data leave the machine?
Structures are fetched from public databases directly by your browser. Your session stays local unless you explicitly export or anchor it.
Do I need a crypto wallet?
No. Wallet sign-in is optional and only attaches an author to a record. Nothing is charged, and nothing is anchored unless you choose to sign it yourself.
Can I self-host it?
Yes — it is a static page plus a small Python server. make serve.
