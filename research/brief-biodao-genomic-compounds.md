### Item 5 — Genomic DAO (S27) + Genome.Blockchain (S28): reframe as consent-gated, federated, GA4GH-aligned — and add the openSNP closure as the cautionary tale
- **Where:** `articles/article-12-genomic-dao.html`, `series/S27-genomic-dao.html`, `articles/article-13-genome-blockchain.html`, `series/S28-genome-blockchain.html`.
- **Verified facts to add:**
  - **GA4GH** (Global Alliance for Genomics and Health): formed **2013**, uniting **500+** leading institutions; develops open technical standards, policy frameworks and tools for voluntary, secure genomic data sharing. Open standards: **Data Use Ontology (DUO)** — used in annotation of **over 200,000 datasets worldwide** (Cell Genomics, Nov 2021, still cited 2026); **GA4GH Passport** for data authorisation/access; data-sharing lexicon ("data visiting", "federated data analysis") finalised **Jul 2025** (Human Genomics 2025, 19:80). Model: "data visiting" — data is analysed inside the provider's computing environment rather than downloaded. GA4GH does not hold genomic data itself.
  - **All of Us (NIH):** **880,000+ participants** from all 50 states; **22,580+ registered researchers** from **1,360+ organisations**; **1,350+ publications** (>500 in 2025); **14.7 million biosample aliquots**; **499,000+ EHRs**; DNA results returned to **733,000+ individuals** (NIH Council of Councils update, 14 May 2026). Whole-genome sequencing data released for **over 245,000 participants** (2025 review). Goal: 1 million participants by end of 2026. Access via the Researcher Workbench (cloud); **no access fee, but user-borne computational costs**. Individual-level genomic data is controlled access, not open download.
    - Source: https://dpcpsi.nih.gov/sites/dpcpsi/files/2026-05/1045AM-May-15-All-of-Us-Update-508.pdf (14 May 2026)
  - **UK Biobank:** **~500,000 volunteers**; whole-genome sequencing of **490,640 participants** (Nature, 2025). Access terms (**Firecrawl-scraped official page, 7 Nov 2026, success=true**): track record of legitimate health-related research, recognised-organisation affiliation, sanctions-compliant country; background checks on every applicant; Access Committee for concerns. **Since Jan 2025, applications from insurance companies for direct access to de-identified data are no longer approved.** Researchers sign a Material Transfer Agreement; **prohibited from trying to re-identify participants and from sharing data with non-collaborators**. Since **2024, access is by default via the UK Biobank Research Analysis Platform (UKB-RAP)**, a restricted cloud platform — downloads are no longer the default — and researchers agree to delete data when the project ends. Tiered access fees apply.
    - Source: https://www.ukbiobank.ac.uk/about-us/how-we-work/access-to-uk-biobank-data/
  - **openSNP — CLOSED:** founded 2011 by Bastian Greshake; users uploaded consumer genotype files under **CC0**; ~13,000 people shared data. **Announced closure 31 Mar 2025; all user data deleted 10 Apr 2025.** Cause: the **23andMe Chapter 11 bankruptcy (23 Mar 2025)** and public fears of DNA misuse. **Do not cite openSNP as a live open-genomics resource.**
    - Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/OpenSNP; http://rip.so/opensnp.html
- **Wording to use:** "Genomic DAO (S27, proposed) is a consent-gated, federated data-sharing model in the GA4GH spirit — data visiting, not open download. Fully open, CC0 individual-level genomic data is no longer regarded as safe to hold: openSNP, which ran that model for 14 years, shut down and deleted all user data in April 2025 after the 23andMe bankruptcy."

### Item 6 — Compound-library pages: add the ChEMBL + PubChem open stack, with source/version/date attribution — and the DrugBank trap
