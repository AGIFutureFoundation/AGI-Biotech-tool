# Research — Bio Research Expansion (Batch 1)

program: ALS, cancer, Alzheimer's, toxicity screening, psychedelic research,
BioDAO/genomic data-sharing, compound entries, patent verification, and the
SMILES/SMARTS reference. Staged from the local bio research expansion package
on 2026-11-07.

## Honesty frame

These are research briefs and literature reviews, not results, filings, or
medical advice. Figures, valuations, and claims are labelled in each document
as verified, sourced, or suggested — do not present suggested figures as
established fact, and do not treat any compound entry as a proven treatment.
Patent status notes are application/publication records; verify current status
before citing them externally.
